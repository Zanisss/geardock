00Top ↑Prep week

Patch 12.1 went live 11 Aug; Season 2 opens 18 Aug. The raid, ranked keys, Bountiful Delves and the Great Vault are all shut until then — but Mythic 0 and Heroic Altar of Fangs are open, and that gear carries into week one. This list disappears when the season starts.

    04Top ↑The Great Vault

    Season 2 rewrote the raid row: every raid vault slot now pays out one difficulty above what you actually killed. A Heroic clear hands you Myth track. This is the single biggest change of the patch, and it means Heroic stays worth clearing all season instead of falling off in week four.

    08Top ↑The weekly routine

    In priority order by reward, not by time. The full list is a long week — eight keys at +10 is an evening on its own. If you have an hour, do the last four: the weekly quest, a Prey hunt, your Bountiful Delves and spending crests return the most per minute.
