00Top ↑The weekly routine

In priority order by reward, not by time. The full list is a long week — eight keys at +10 is an evening on its own. If you only have an hour: Azta'rec, your Trovehunter's Bounty, and spending crests before reset. Those three return the most per minute of anything on this page.

    Not Delves only. Every world activity maps onto one shared tier ladder: Delves T1–11, Ritual Sites T1–6, Prey at T1/5/8 by difficulty, world events at T1. Each slot takes the tier of the activity that filled it, so a single Tier 8 clear does not lift the other two — it takes eight Tier 8+ activities to make all three choices 305. Tier 8 is the ceiling: Tiers 9–11 are harder without paying more here.

    +10 is the number that matters. Below +10 your dungeon vault tops out at Hero 4/6 (315). At +10 it becomes Myth 1/6 (318) and stays there no matter how high you push. If you only get one thing out of this page: eight keys at +10 every week.
    Nebulous Voidcore as a vault pick. From your second vault of the season onward, with any three slots filled, you can take a bonus roll instead of an item. Worth it when all nine slots are junk for your spec.

    07Top ↑Catalyst, Venomstones and crafting

    The systems that move gear sideways rather than up the track. All three are slow-drip resources — the mistakes are spending them early on something you replace, or forgetting they exist until week ten.

    The Catalystlive now
    Charge currencyVenomblight Manaflux
    Convertible slotsHead, Shoulder, Chest, Hands, Legs
    Minimum gearVeteran track or higher, PvE only
    WhereThe Catalyst, Silvermoon
    AccrualOne every two weeks, automatic, account-wide
    Charge cap8
    Bonus chargeMidnight Season 2: Serpent Scion — Ula'tek on Heroic or Mythic, or 2000 M+ rating, or 1600 rated PvP. Once per character.
    Extra dropsAfter Catalyst Unbound (your 4-set): Manaflux also drops from Mythic+, Venomous Abyss and Tidebound Grotto bosses, Bountiful Delves and rated PvP
    The change that matters this season: converted pieces keep the original item's secondary stats, gem sockets and any cantrip effect. Tier no longer forces you onto fixed secondaries, so a well-itemised drop stays well-itemised after conversion. Venomcursed armour from Ula'tek survives conversion with its effect intact. Second change, easy to miss: the biweekly drip is no longer the only supply. Finish your 4-set and Catalyst Unbound turns Manaflux into a drop from content you were already running — which makes rushing the 4-set worth more than it looks.
